## 4. Accessibility — PARTIAL (contrast)

Contrast ratios computed against the new palette (WCAG AA: 4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large/UI):

| Pair | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| bone on soil (body) | 15.03:1 | **PASS** |
| bone-dim on soil (secondary) | 10.05:1 | **PASS** |
| bone on wine (button label) | 6.36:1 | **PASS** |
| bone / bone-dim on soil-2 (cards) | 13.7 / 9.2:1 | **PASS** |
| **wine on soil (text/links)** | **2.36:1** | **FAIL** |
| moss on soil (eyebrows, small) | 3.70:1 | **PASS (large/UI) / under AA for small text** |
| line on soil (hairline) | 1.43:1 | Decorative — acceptable but very subtle |

- **BLOCKER — wine as text on dark (2.36:1).** Used for: Events date/time (`Events.tsx:44`), newsletter success messages (`Newsletter.tsx:57`, `FooterNewsletter.tsx:30`), and hover states (`contact`, `press`, `books/[slug]` `hover:text-brand`). Fix: reserve wine for **backgrounds/buttons**; use bone (or a lightened wine, e.g. brightened ~1.5×) for **text/links/hover** on soil.
- **Minor — moss eyebrows (3.70:1).** Fine as bold/large, but small uppercase labels ideally ≥4.5:1. Consider lightening `moss` slightly.
- Reduced motion honored (intro + former smoke). Inputs have `aria-label`s. Focus rings come from shadcn defaults — verify visible focus on the wine pill/nav.
