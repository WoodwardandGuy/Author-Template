## 3. Account & Billing Structure

### How agency-managed Google Ads accounts work

Our agency operates a **Manager Account** (sometimes called an MCC — "My Client Center"). It's a single login that lets us manage Google Ads for multiple clients from one place without needing your password. Think of it like a building manager who has keys to every unit but lives on-site in their own office.

### Ownership

**You own your Google Ads account.** This is important and it is not how all agencies do things. The account, all campaign data, conversion history, and creative assets belong to Tree Professionals of Harrisburg. We manage it on your behalf under our Manager Account.

Formal ownership statement for your records:

> *Google Ads account `753-718-2468` was created by Woodward & Guy on behalf of Tree Professionals of Harrisburg and is owned by Tree Professionals of Harrisburg, managed under Woodward & Guy's Manager Account (`432-245-4246`).*

### Billing

**You are billed directly by Google.** Your credit card goes on your Google Ads account, and Google charges you directly for ad spend — we do not act as a middleman for ad spend. This keeps things clean:

- You see every dollar Google charges, in real time, on your card statement
- Ad spend is your business expense, straight from your books — no commingling with agency invoices
- If our relationship ever ends, the account, history, and billing stay intact with you

Our management fee is invoiced separately by Woodward & Guy (see Section 10).

### Access

- **You (Lyle) have Admin access** to the Google Ads account, invited when the account was created. This is a safety measure: if anything ever happens to our Manager Account relationship, you retain full control of the account.
- **We (Woodward & Guy) run day-to-day operations** through our Manager Account. You do not need to log in to Google Ads — ever. We handle everything.

## 4. Campaign Strategy

A high-level preview of what the launch will look like. We will walk you through the specifics before going live.

- **Search campaigns** targeting high-intent local queries people type when they need a tree service right now:
  - "tree removal Harrisburg PA"
  - "emergency tree service"
  - "stump grinding near me"
  - "tree service Mechanicsburg" (and your other service areas)
- **Landing pages** — we send clicks to the relevant page already built on your site (`/services/tree-removal`, `/services/emergency-service`, `/areas/harrisburg`, etc.). Each has a clear call-to-action and is set up to convert form fills and phone calls.
- **Ad extensions** — these are the extra links, phone numbers, and trust signals that show up below your ads. Planned extensions:
  - **Call extension** — your phone number shows up directly in the ad (one tap to call on mobile)
  - **Sitelink extensions** — direct links to Services, Areas Served, About, Contact
  - **Callout extensions** — "Licensed & Insured," "Free Estimates," "24/7 Emergency Service," "Local & Family Owned" (or whatever you'd like to emphasize)
- **Geographic targeting** — Harrisburg plus a 30-mile radius, or specifically the service areas you already have pages for (Mechanicsburg, Camp Hill, Carlisle, Enola, Hershey, Lemoyne, New Cumberland, Middletown, Colonial Park, Hummelstown, Steelton).
- **Call-only ads on mobile** — these ads only show on phones and, when tapped, place a call directly to you. Extremely effective for emergency tree service queries.

## 6. Recommended Starting Budget

We recommend starting with **$500–$1,000 per month in Google Ads spend**.

**Why this range:**
- Enough to gather meaningful conversion data within ~30 days — we need real click and lead volume to see what's working before we optimize
- Appropriate for a first-time advertiser — conservative enough that you can evaluate ROI honestly before committing more
- Can be scaled up any time without restarting anything — we just raise the budget and campaigns keep running

**What this will realistically get you** (order-of-magnitude, not a guarantee — actual numbers depend on competition, seasonality, and your quality score):
- ~500–1,000 clicks per month to your landing pages
- ~15–40 lead form submissions and phone calls per month (typical 3–5% conversion rate for tree service)
- Cost per lead typically $25–$65 for local trades in your area

**Important:** Ad spend is separate from our management fee. Google charges your card directly for ad spend; we invoice you separately for our management work. See Section 10.

### Ongoing (Month 2+)
- Monthly optimization, reporting, and strategy review
- Quarterly strategic planning — should we shift budget, test new services, expand geos, etc.
