6.Recommended Starting Budget

We recommend starting with a Google Ads media budget of $1,000 to $2,500 per month. That range produces enough conversion data within the first thirty days to judge performance honestly and begin optimizing. Lower budgets are workable but produce data slowly; higher budgets are appropriate after the first ninety days once performance is known.

As an order-of-magnitude estimate at the recommended spend, we expect 500 to 1,000 clicks per month, 15 to 40 leads per month (form submissions and phone calls combined), and a cost per lead in the range of $35 to $65, subject to competition, seasonality, and quality score. These figures are directional, not guaranteed.

Ad spend and management fees are two separate invoices. Google charges your card directly for ad spend. Woodward & Guy invoices you separately for management. See Section 10 for the full fee structure.
